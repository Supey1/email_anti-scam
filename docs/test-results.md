### Legitimate cases that WERE flagged (11)
- **L-E04** Google password reset ("verify your identity") → warn: It says something is wrong with your account. This is a common trick to get you to log in on a fake page. It asks about private information like account numbers
- **L-E06** Friend's newsletter with a shortened link → warn: A link is shortened, which hides where it really goes.
- **L-E07** Grandchild mentions a gift card for a birthday → warn: It mentions gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto. Scammers ask for these because the money can't be gotten back.
- **L-E08** Doctor's office reminder → warn: It tries to rush you. Real companies give you time to think.
- **L-E09** Real Netflix payment reminder → warn: It says something is wrong with your account. This is a common trick to get you to log in on a fake page.
- **L-E10** Real UPS delivery notice → warn: It says a package couldn't be delivered. Fake delivery notices are a very common scam. Check the tracking number on the shipper's real website instead.
- **L-E11** Church volunteer sign-up, urgent → warn: It tries to rush you. Real companies give you time to think.
- **L-E12** Utility bill mentioning an account number → warn: It asks about private information like account numbers or passwords. Never send these by email.
- **L-E13** News digest whose tracking links hide the destination → warn: A link says it goes to nytimes.com, but it really goes to links.dailydigest.example.
- **L-E14** Real Chase fraud alert → warn: It says something is wrong with your account. This is a common trick to get you to log in on a fake page.
- **L-A05** Ad whose link text shows a company site but goes through an ad tracker → warn: In an advertisement: a link says it goes to shoes.example, but it really goes to ad.doubleclick.net.
