Which websites to check

To stop a scam link before it opens, the extension looks at the page you're on, the link you click, and the email you open. It does this on this computer: nothing you read or type is sent anywhere.

You can change this any time, here or in Chrome's extension settings.

What we keep, on this computer only

  • These settings, and the websites you've said are safe.
  • The list of known dangerous websites.
  • Until you close the browser: the warnings shown on our warning page (the link and the reasons).

What we never do

  • We never keep your emails, the pages you read, or anything you type.
  • We never ask for your cookies or your browsing history.
  • We send nothing to anyone. (Checking links with an online safety service is not turned on.)
